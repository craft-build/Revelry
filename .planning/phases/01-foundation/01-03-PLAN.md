| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-11 | S | Persona create/update | mitigate | Rust validates name is non-empty, strips leading/trailing whitespace, enforces max length (255 chars) |

| T-01-15 | I | Persona block output | accept | Formatted for AI prompt consumption, no sensitive data beyond what user intentionally entered |
